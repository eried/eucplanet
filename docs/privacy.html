Privacy Policy

EUC Planet for Android and iPhone · Last updated 2026-11-17

EUC Planet is an open-source companion app for electric unicycles. It is published and maintained by Erwin Ried. This page describes what the app accesses on your device and what happens to that data. It is the same on Android and on iPhone unless a line says otherwise.

Short version

  • The app runs on your phone. It needs no account and rides fine with no internet connection.
  • It does not contain advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • Trip recordings, settings, and telemetry stay on your device unless you choose to send them somewhere: a cloud folder you own, the public leaderboard, or a live share with friends. All three are off until you turn them on.
  • Maps, routing, weather and charging stations ask public services for what they show. The app adds no account or device identifier to those requests.

What the app accesses

  • Bluetooth: to scan for and connect to your wheel and the accessories you pair (buttons, radar, tire sensors, an external GPS).
  • Precise location (GPS): for GPS speed, trip recording, navigation, the weather where you are and the automatic lights. Location is never used for advertising or identification, and it leaves the phone only as listed under What goes over the internet.
  • Notifications: to show the ongoing connection and deliver alarm alerts.
  • Running in the background: to keep the wheel connection and GPS tracking alive while the screen is off during a ride.
  • Camera + microphone (opt-in): when you open Overlay Studio, a video and photo recorder with a live telemetry overlay, and the camera when you scan a QR code. The captured files land in your own device storage (on iPhone, your photo library) and the app never streams them anywhere. On Android the microphone also hears voice commands, through Android's own speech recognition, only after you start listening.
  • Photo library (iPhone): add only, to save what Overlay Studio records. The app cannot read your library.
  • Local network: to find a helmet HUD on your WiFi and send it live ride data.
  • Cloud folder (optional): if you enable sync, the app reads and writes inside one folder you choose: a folder in your own Dropbox, or on Android any folder from the file picker (Google Drive, OneDrive, local, etc.). You can revoke access at any time.

What goes over the internet

  • Maps, routes and places: map tiles, route solving, place search, charging stations and points of interest come from public services built on OpenStreetMap, and from Open Charge Map. A request carries the area or the coordinates it asks about, nothing else. Offline routing, once downloaded, stays on the phone.
  • Weather: the forecast is asked from Open-Meteo or MET Norway for the place you are in.
  • Cloud sync (opt-in): a copy of your trips, settings, themes and overlays goes to the folder you chose. That folder belongs to you; the developer has no access to it.
  • EUC Stats leaderboard (opt-in): if you join, the trips you upload and the profile you create go to eucstats.ried.no, run by the developer. Your display name, country flag, avatar and overall riding stats become public there. Locations are clustered into wide areas of the map and raw GPS tracks are never published. The app shows this list before you join, and you can unlink at any time.
  • Ride together (opt-in): while you share a ride, your position passes through a relay to the people who have your link or QR code. It is end-to-end encrypted: the relay only passes sealed packets along and cannot read them. It stops when you stop sharing.
  • Sharing a route: the link or QR code holds the stops itself. Nothing is uploaded.

What is collected or stored

  • On your device: your app settings, the Bluetooth address of the wheels and accessories you connected (so auto-connect works), and any trip CSV files you record.
  • In your cloud folder (optional, only if you enable sync): a copy of your app settings, themes, overlays and trip files.
  • On the leaderboard (optional, only if you join): your profile and the trips you uploaded.

What is not collected

  • No email, no phone number, no password. The only profile is the leaderboard one, and only if you join.
  • No analytics events, crash reports, or usage metrics sent off-device.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No contact list, call log or SMS. The app never reads your photos: it only gets a picture you pick yourself, such as a leaderboard avatar.
  • The camera and microphone are only opened by the features above, while you are using them. Footage stays on your device.

Permissions explained: Android

  • BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT: required to find and control the wheel. The scan is flagged neverForLocation.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: GPS speed, trip recording, navigation, weather and the automatic lights.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION, REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: to keep the wheel connection and GPS running in the background while you ride.
  • CAMERA, RECORD_AUDIO: Overlay Studio, QR codes and voice commands. Each prompt appears the first time you use the feature and can be revoked at any time in Android Settings.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: to show the connection status and alerts.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: optional, to draw your HUD layout on top of other apps. You grant it yourself in system settings.
  • VIBRATE: for alarm haptic feedback.
  • CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: to find a helmet HUD on your WiFi.
  • INTERNET: for what is listed under What goes over the internet.

Permissions explained: iPhone

  • Bluetooth: required to find and control the wheel.
  • Location (While Using, or Always): GPS speed, trip recording, navigation, weather and the automatic lights. Always lets a trip keep recording while the screen is off.
  • Camera, Microphone: Overlay Studio and QR codes. Each prompt appears the first time you use the feature.
  • Photos (add only): to save what Overlay Studio records.
  • Local Network: to find a helmet HUD on your WiFi.
  • Notifications: for the alerts you turn on, such as a finished charge.

Every one of them can be revoked at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored in the app's private data directory. Trip files that you exported or synced to your own cloud folder remain under your control. A leaderboard profile and its uploads stay on eucstats.ried.no after you unlink; to have them removed, ask through the contact below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Open source

The full source code is public at github.com/eried/eucplanet, so the claims above can be independently verified.

About this website

This policy covers the app, on Android and on iPhone. The marketing website at eucplanet.ried.no loads Microsoft Clarity so the developer can see which pages and links people actually use. Clarity records page visits, clicks, and anonymised session replays. It does not run inside the app and has no access to your wheel, your rides, or your device data. You can opt out with any standard tracker blocker.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/eried/eucplanet/issues.
