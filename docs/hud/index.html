EUC Planet HUD

A helmet display that mirrors your phone over WiFi.

Download HUD APK

Install

  1. Sideload hud-<version>.apk on the HUD device. Works on MotoEye E6, generic tablets, spare phones.
  2. Install phone-<version>-slim.apk on your phone, or get it from Google Play.

Pair

  1. Turn on the phone's hotspot and connect the HUD to it, or put both on the same WiFi.
  2. Launch the HUD app.
  3. On the phone, open Settings → Integrations → Motoeye HUD and turn on Enable data link.

That is the whole setup. The phone finds the HUD by itself and telemetry starts within a second or two. There is no IP to type.

If it does not find the HUD

The phone tries four channels in order, and the settings screen names the one that worked:

  1. UDP beacon from the HUD. The most reliable, and the only one that survives a hotspot that blocks multicast.
  2. mDNS, browsing for _eucplanet._tcp.local.
  3. Last known address, kept as a hint from the previous session rather than as the only truth.
  4. Subnet scan of the phone's own network, which takes a few seconds and only runs once the first three come up empty.

If all four fail, turn off Discover link address automatically and type the IP the HUD shows on its splash screen. Keep both sides on matching versions.
