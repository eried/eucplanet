EUC Planet HUD

A helmet display that mirrors your phone over WiFi.

Download HUD APK

Install

  1. Sideload hud-<version>.apk on the HUD device. Works on MotoEye E6, generic tablets, spare phones.
  2. Install EUC Planet on your phone from eucplanet.ried.no.

Pair

  1. Turn on the phone's hotspot and connect the HUD to it, or put both on the same WiFi.
  2. Launch the HUD app.
  3. On the phone, open Settings → Integrations → Motoeye HUD and turn on Enable data link.

That is the whole setup. The phone finds the HUD by itself.

How it finds the HUD

Four ways, tried in this order.

  1. 1 UDP beacon Most reliable

    The HUD calls out and the phone hears it. The only one that survives a hotspot that blocks multicast.

  2. 2 mDNS

    The phone browses the network for _eucplanet._tcp.local.

  3. 3 Last known address

    Where the HUD was last session.

  4. 4 Subnet scan Last resort

    A sweep of the phone's own network. It takes a few seconds.

If all four fail, set Discovery mode to Fixed and type the IP the HUD shows on its splash screen. Keep both sides on matching versions.
