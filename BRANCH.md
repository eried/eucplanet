# i18n/app-native-pass

A review branch on top of next-experimental: every language of the phone app
reread so it sounds like a rider wrote it. Nothing else changes.

## Worked on

- All 29 languages of the phone app were reread, string by string: about 6,200 texts reworded where they sounded translated, stiff or wrong. Buttons, names and voice commands were left alone unless they were mistakes.
- The same thing now has the same name across each language (for example one word for "unlink", one for "battery pack").

## Please test

**Anyone who reads one of the 29 languages natively**
- Set the app to your language and walk through the dashboard, Settings, the trip recorder and the navigator. Does anything read odd, wrong or cut off?
- Does a button or tab no longer fit its space?
- Voice reports and voice commands: do they still sound right and still understand you?
