Your tire sensor now reaches the whole app: dashboard tile, alarms, trip graphs, overlay and HUD, not only the settings row. A flat tire reads 0 rather than looking like a sensor that stopped, so a low pressure alarm can actually fire.

Battery (est) is a dashboard tile too, not just an alarm, and it now follows your percentage override the way every other reading does.

Tire pressure shows in the unit you picked, psi, bar, kPa, kgf/cm2 or MPa, on every screen.
