"EUC Planet is the open-source companion for your electric unicycle: live telemetry, navigation, video overlays, and watch, widget and HUD support in one app. No ads, no subscription, no account, and nothing leaves your phone. Buy it here to support development, or grab it for free on GitHub.\n\nLIVE TELEMETRY\n• Big speed dial with an optional color band on your safe / warn / danger thresholds.\n• Battery, voltage, current, PWM, temperature, trip and odometer, each with a history graph.\n• Pick which metrics fill the dashboard and build your own quick actions.\n• Metric, Imperial or a mix.\n\nNAVIGATOR\n• Multi-stop routes on a map, with a full-path mode: tap anywhere along the route to detour around sand, gravel or closures.\n• Voice guidance, off-route recovery, and a Treasure Hunt mode for unmarked spots.\n• Routes survive an app kill.\n\nOVERLAY STUDIO\n• Record phone-camera video with a tunable telemetry overlay: dials, gauges, graphs, text, mini-map and layered cameras.\n• Export a finished MP4, or a transparent overlay to composite over GoPro / drone footage.\n\nON YOUR WRIST\n• Wear OS: full-bleed dial, wheel / phone / watch batteries, and horn / light / voice controls. Tested on Galaxy Watch Ultra.\n• Garmin: speed, battery, PWM, temperature and distance across 138 Connect IQ watches.\n\nMORE WAYS TO SEE IT\n• Home-screen widgets and a floating picture-in-picture window.\n• Phone HUD over other apps, or a helmet HUD streamed to a Motoeye E6.\n• Rear-view radar: Garmin Varia shows approaching traffic and closing speed.\n\nALARMS & VOICE\n• Your own threshold alarms on speed, battery, temperature, PWM, voltage or current, with beeps, voice and vibration.\n• Spoken reports, event announcements, and an optional engine sound that scales with speed.\n\nTRIP RECORDING\n• GPS + telemetry in DarknessBot-compatible CSV, auto-recorded on connection.\n• Compare any past ride against the live one, side by side.\n\nINTEGRATIONS\n• Flic 2 buttons and volume keys for hands-free control.\n• External BLE GPS (RaceBox) for centimetre-class speed and altitude.\n\nWHEEL SUPPORT\nVerified: InMotion V14 (50GB / 50S), P6, V1 family (V5 / V8 / V10). Preliminary: InMotion V12. Preview (from public specs, testers wanted): KingSong, Begode, Veteran and Ninebot families. Help add yours: connect and file a wheel report from the in-app banner.\n\nBug reports: github.com/eried/eucplanet/issues\nIdeas: ideas.ried.no/euc-planet\nTelegram: t.me/EUCPlanetApp\nSource: github.com/eried/eucplanet"
